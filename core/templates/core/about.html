About Leviticals

Set apart for the ministry of the Word, worship, and the proclamation of the Gospel.

Who We Are

Leviticals is a Christian media ministry committed to broadcasting the Gospel of Jesus Christ through the written Word, music, and digital media. We are named after the Levites — the tribe set apart in Israel for the ministry of the sanctuary, the keepers of the Word, the worshippers before the Lord.

We believe the same calling rests on the Church today: to be a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people set apart to proclaim the excellencies of Him who called us out of darkness into His marvellous light. — 1 Peter 2:9

What We Believe

We believe that the Gospel of Jesus Christ is the only solution to the problems of humanity — personal, social, national, and global. Every article, devotional, song, and broadcast we produce is grounded in this conviction.

✦

The Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority in all matters of faith and conduct.

✦

Jesus Christ is Lord — fully God and fully man, crucified for our sins, risen from the dead, and coming again.

✦

Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone — not by works.

✦

The Great Commission is the mandate of every believer — to go, to disciple, to baptise, to teach.

Our Vision

To build a digital sanctuary where believers are equipped with the Word, worshippers find Gospel music that stirs their faith, and seekers encounter the living Christ through every piece of content we publish.

We envision Leviticals becoming a trusted voice in Christian media — not the loudest, but the most faithful.
